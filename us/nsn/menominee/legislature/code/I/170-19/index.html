Code of the Menominee Indian Tribe

§ 170-19 Definitions.

A. "Adverse action" means refusing to hire, refusing to license, suspending or terminating employment, suspending or revoking a license, placing conditions on hiring or licensing.

B. "Cannabis" means all cannabinoids from the plant Cannabis sativa L. including delta-9 tetrahydrocannabinol.

C. "Employee" means

(1) A person who performs work for an employer for monetary compensation who is not an independent contractor or an appointed or elected official;

(2) An applicant seeking to become an employee of an employer;

(3) A person licensed by a licensing entity;

(4) An applicant for a license with a licensing entity.

D. "Employer" means:

(1) The Menominee Indian Tribe of Wisconsin;

(2) The businesses of the Tribe chartered pursuant to Article XIII of the Tribal Constitution;

(3) Menominee Tribal Enterprises; and

(4) College of Menominee Nation.

E. "Licensing entity" means any commission, agency, or employee of the Menominee Indian Tribe that issues licenses to persons required for a person's employment by an Employer.

F. "Reasonable suspicion" means a good-faith, objective belief—based on specific, articulable facts and observations—that an employee while on duty may be under the influence of drugs in a way that could impair their ability to perform their job safely or effectively.

G. "Safety-sensitive position" means: a position requiring a commercial driver’s license or requiring an employee to operate a motor vehicle for which state or federal law requires drug or alcohol testing of a job applicant or an employee; a position where the person is authorized to enforce the laws of the Tribe pursuant to Chapter 132 of the Menominee Tribal Code; any other position which an employer deems to be safety-sensitive pursuant to a written policy if such written policy has been:

(1) Implemented after the effective date of final approval of this Article III; and

(2) Approved by the Menominee Tribal Legislature.

(Ord. 25-66.)
